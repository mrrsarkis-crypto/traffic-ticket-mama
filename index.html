✨ Questions

Everything You're Wondering

Is this a law firm?

No. Traffic Ticket Mama is a document-preparation and case-tracking service. We're not a law firm and don't give legal advice. A California Certified Paralegal and licensed attorneys review your file.

Can you guarantee my ticket gets dismissed?

No honest service can. What we guarantee: a licensed attorney reviews every defense, we file everything correctly and on time, and we fight hard.

How does the free scan work?

Upload a photo of your ticket. Our AI extracts violation codes, court info, and deadlines in seconds. Then we tell you whether fighting makes sense — free, no pressure.

Do I have to go to court?

No. Trial by Written Declaration means everything is handled in writing. No court appearance, no time off work.

What if I missed my court date?

We handle Failure to Appear cases ($299) — clearing the FTA and fighting the underlying ticket.
