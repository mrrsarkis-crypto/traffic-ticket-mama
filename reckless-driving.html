Reckless & Careless Driving (VC 23103)

Reckless driving is more than a ticket — it can add 2 points and, in some cases, becomes a misdemeanor. We help you review the citation and prepare a written defense.

Why reckless driving needs real attention

Reckless driving (VC 23103) in California can add 2 points to your driving record, raise your insurance, and in some cases becomes a misdemeanor. For commercial drivers, even one "reportable" conviction can threaten a CDL. Many citations are eligible for reduction or dismissal — but only where defects on the written citation are properly raised and the court agrees. We do not guarantee any outcome.

Defects we look for

  • Elements of the charge — whether the citation actually alleges the required elements of "willful or wanton" disregard.
  • Officer observation — continuous, accurate observation required to support VC 23103.
  • Witness / evidence chain — collisions often hinge on witness statements that may be incomplete or missing.
  • Code section accuracy — whether VC 23103 vs. 23103.5 (alcohol-related) is correctly cited.
  • Factual precision — the written facts must match the charge — mismatches are a classic defense.

How written-declaration defense works

We prepare a written declaration that organizes your statement and any evidence, focused on the elements the court must prove.

We help prepare documents and do not appear in court. For a potential misdemeanor, consider whether independent legal advice is appropriate for your situation.
