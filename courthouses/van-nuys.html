What to Expect at the Van Nuys Courthouse

The Van Nuys Courthouse sits on Sylmar Avenue in the heart of the San Fernando Valley, a short walk from Van Nuys City Hall and the nearby transit center. If your citation is assigned here, your weekday window around Van Nuys Blvd and Sylmar Ave can get busy with people checking court dates, paying fines, and appearing for traffic arraignments — so arriving early in the morning is the smoothest bet.

When you arrive, expect a security screening at the entrance. Leave large bags, pocket knives, and anything on the prohibited list at home or in a locked vehicle to move through the line quickly. Courtrooms are on the upper floors; the self-service lobbies on the ground level handle traffic-ticket lookups and kiosk payments.

Handling a Traffic Citation Here

For most traffic matters, you have three paths before your appearance date: pay the fine in full (a conviction is entered on your record), request traffic school online if you remain eligible, or appear to contest the citation. You can also check whether your matter qualifies for a payment plan directly on the court's portal rather than letting it go to collection.

The full prefix on a Van Nuys citation tells the court where it was filed. If your ticket was issued on one of the busy corridors like Sepulveda, Victory, or the 405/101 interchanges, it is common to be directed here. Confirm your exact courtroom and department on the official LA Court site before the date, because department assignments change.

A Practical Checklist Before Your Date

Write down your citation number and your driver's license number, and bring a photo ID. If you plan to ask about traffic school or a payment plan, bring documents that support your request — but keep your expectations realistic, because eligibility is decided by the court. If you are unsure whether you must appear or simply pay, call the court and ask the clerk. A missed appearance can lead to a failure-to-appear penalty, which is far more expensive than handling the original ticket.
